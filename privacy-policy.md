# Privacy Policy: Spoiler Shield for GTA 6

_Effective date: November 18, 2026_

Spoiler Shield for GTA 6 ("the extension") is an unofficial, fan-made browser extension that blurs or hides GTA 6 spoilers on YouTube, Reddit, X/Twitter and Google Search.

## Data collection

The extension **does not collect, store on any server, sell, share, or transmit any personal or non-personal data.**

- No accounts, analytics, tracking, cookies, advertising or third-party services.
- The extension makes no network requests of its own.

## How page content is used

On supported sites, the extension reads the text of the page **locally in your browser** to check it against your spoiler keywords, and adds a blur overlay where needed. This text never leaves your device and is not saved.

## Settings

Your settings (on/off switch, blur or hide mode, keyword lists) are saved with Chrome's built-in `chrome.storage.sync` storage. If you have Chrome sync turned on, Google syncs these settings across your own Chrome browsers as part of Chrome itself. The developer has no access to them. Uninstalling the extension removes them.

## Permissions

- **storage**: saves the settings described above.
- **Access to youtube.com, reddit.com, x.com, twitter.com and Google Search pages**: lets the extension read page text on those sites, locally, to detect and hide spoilers.
